Thank you for reviewing Muza Watch. Answers below.

1) THIRD-PARTY ANALYTICS: No. The app contains no analytics SDK of any kind — no Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Segment or Facebook SDK — and no event-tracking code anywhere. No usage, behavioural or device data is collected for analytics by us or anyone else.

2) THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING: No. No advertising SDK, no advertising of any kind, and the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is not used. We do not intend to introduce advertising. As no advertising exists in the app, no ad network policies apply.

3) DATA SHARED WITH THIRD PARTIES: Only the service providers needed to run the app, each for one stated purpose. None receives data for advertising, profiling or analytics. We do not sell personal information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

- Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: receives only an anonymous device push token, to notify users of new titles. It receives no phone number, name, or record of what was watched. Notifications are optional and can be disabled in device settings.
- Google Cast (Chromecast): used only if a user chooses to cast to their own TV. It discovers devices on the local network and passes the video URL to the chosen device. No personal information is sent.
- Cloudflare Stream: hosts and delivers the video. It holds no account information.
- Africa's Talking / Twilio (SMS): receive the phone number solely to deliver the one-time sign-in code, for the seconds delivery takes. Not used for marketing; consent data is never shared or sold.
- Stripe / Yo! Payments (Mobile Money): handle payment credentials directly if a user subscribes. Card numbers and mobile-money PINs never reach our servers; we receive only whether payment succeeded.

Account data is stored on our own servers in Germany (Munich), within the EU. Traffic is encrypted in transit, and sign-in codes and session tokens are stored hashed, never in readable form.

4) OTHER DATA COLLECTED: Only what the app needs to function:
- Phone number — required; it is the account identifier and how the sign-in code is delivered.
- Display name, email, postal address — optional, stored only if the user enters them.
- Playback position and titles played — so viewing resumes and Continue Watching works.
- Saved list — titles the user chose to save.
- Subscription records — which plan was bought and when, to grant access. No card details.
- Device label, session time, push token — to enforce the limit on simultaneous devices and streams, and to deliver notifications if allowed.

None of this is used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and none is sold.

COMPLIANCE WITH GUIDELINE 1.3:
- Accounts are created and held by an adult. Sign-up requires a mobile phone number, and subscribing requires a payment method — a child cannot complete either. Children watch using a parent's or guardian's account. The app never asks a child for personal information and does not knowingly collect any from a child.
- No advertising anywhere in the app, so nothing about a child's viewing is used for advertising or to build an advertising profile.
- No chat, no comments, no user-uploaded content, and no way for one viewer to contact another.
- Purchases require an adult's Mobile Money account or payment card, confirmed outside the app.
- Viewing history belongs to the account holder, not to a named child, and can be cleared or deleted on request.
- Parents may request access to or deletion of all data held for their account by emailing sales@prosystemsug.com.

Our privacy policy states all of the above and is publicly available without a login at https://ham.sentepos.com/privacy
